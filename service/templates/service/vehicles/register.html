Register customer vehicles in the system to track their service history. Each vehicle can have multiple work orders.

Required Fields

  • Make: Vehicle manufacturer (e.g., Maruti, Hyundai)

Optional Fields

  • VIN: Vehicle Identification Number for tracking
  • Color: Vehicle color
