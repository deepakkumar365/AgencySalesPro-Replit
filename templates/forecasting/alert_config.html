How Alert Thresholds Work
Shortage Alerts:

An alert is triggered when the forecasted demand exceeds current stock, AND either:

  • The shortage quantity is greater than or equal to the Shortage Quantity Threshold, OR
  • The shortage percentage (shortage / forecast × 100) is greater than or equal to the Shortage Percentage Threshold
Excess Stock Alerts:

An alert is triggered when the current stock exceeds forecasted demand, AND either:

  • The excess quantity is greater than or equal to the Excess Quantity Threshold, OR
  • The excess percentage (excess / forecast × 100) is greater than or equal to the Excess Percentage Threshold
Category-Specific Configurations:

You can create different alert thresholds for specific product categories. Category-specific configurations take precedence over the default (all categories) configuration.

Email Alerts:

When email alerts are enabled and recipients are configured, the system will automatically send email notifications when alerts are triggered during forecast refresh.
